## Multi-Class Prediction of Obesity Risk

**Origin:**  
- **Author:** Maserati Urm  
- **Source:** [Kaggle Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/maseratiurm/multi-class-prediction-of-obesity-risk)  
- **Title:** Multi-Class Prediction of Obesity Risk  
- **Year:** 2023

**Description:**  
This dataset is designed for predicting obesity risk based on various health and demographic factors. It includes multiple classes representing different levels of obesity risk, making it suitable for multi-class classification tasks.
